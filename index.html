How to get help

Something doesn't work?

Use “Report a problem” on the product card — it opens an issue form that asks for your version and system, so we can fix it faster.

Have an idea or a question?

Share it in Discussions: ideas, wallpaper requests and Q&A. Vote 👍 for the ideas you like.

In English, please

Issues are kept in English so everyone can find them — a machine translation is fine. Email works in English, Russian or Ukrainian.

Private matter?

Copyright requests: use “Report image” on the wallpaper page. Everything else: m@mijuru.dev.
